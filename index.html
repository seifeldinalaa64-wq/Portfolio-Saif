About Me

I'm a senior Business Administration student at Galala University, enrolled in a Dual Degree Program with Arizona State University, with a growing focus on business development, entrepreneurship, and project development. Through academic projects, competitions, and leadership activities, I've gained hands-on experience in identifying opportunities, developing projects, working in teams, and turning ideas into practical solutions. I contributed to Dark Storm, an educational project that won 1st place in a competition organized by Saed Foundation, and to LABVERSE, an AI-powered educational platform that secured 3rd place in the RALLY Egypt Competition at Galala University. Alongside my business background, I work in graphic design, video editing, motion graphics, and digital content creation, and I'm currently building my Adobe Illustrator and After Effects skills through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI). I'm looking to contribute to business development, entrepreneurship, project development, digital media, and creative projects while gaining practical experience on meaningful work.
